Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

Telly is a TV and film tracker with a spoiler-safe social layer. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account info: your email address, your handle, and anything optional you add — display name, bio, avatar.
  • Your activity: what you mark as watched, your ratings, tags, and comments, and who you follow.
  • A push token — only if you turn on notifications. You can turn this off at any time in Settings.
  • Crash reports — when the app hits an error, we receive a diagnostic report: the device model, the OS and app version, and an anonymous installation identifier. It doesn't include your email, your account, or anything you've watched.

We don't collect anything beyond what's needed to run the app and fix it when it breaks. There's no advertising, no ad tracking, and nothing here is used to profile you across other apps or websites.

Why we collect it

To run Telly: tracking what you watch, keeping comments spoiler-safe against your own progress, and sending the notifications you've opted into.

Who we share it with

  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and backend. They process data on our behalf; they don't use it for their own purposes.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database) — supplies show/movie metadata and artwork. We send search queries to look up titles; we don't send your personal data to them. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • Apple / Google — only if you choose "Continue with Apple" or "Continue with Google" to sign in.
  • Push notification services (Apple/Google/Expo) — only if you enable notifications, so we can deliver them.
  • Sentry — receives the crash reports described above so faults can be diagnosed. It's configured not to send personal information. As part of how the service works, Sentry derives an approximate location (city and country) from the connecting IP address.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone else.

Your rights

  • Delete your account: any time, in-app, from Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently and immediately deletes your account and everything tied to it — watch history, ratings, comments, follows. It cannot be undone.
  • Access your data: email telly.app.support@gmail.com and we'll get you a copy. There's no self-serve export yet, so this is handled by hand for now — we'll respond promptly.

How long we keep it

For as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is deleted immediately along with it — nothing lingers.

Children

Telly isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under that age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be noted in-app or by email.

Questions, requests, or reports: telly.app.support@gmail.com
